How We Care Works

From abroad or across town, booking trusted assistance for your parents takes under two minutes.

1

Tell Us What's Needed

2

Matched with a Verified Care Companion

A background-checked, eldercare-trained companion in their neighborhood is assigned. At the door, a randomized 4-digit security PIN is verified before entry.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking dignified care for your loved ones.

Companions are dedicated personal navigators who accompany parents to hospitals, doctor OPDs, banks, and grocery runs, while providing warm social engagement, technology support, and medication organization. They do not perform invasive medical procedures (such as injections, intravenous lines, or catheter care).
When a booking is confirmed, a random 4-digit code is sent to your phone and visible to your parent. When the companion arrives at their residence, they must enter this exact code into their companion app to unlock the session. This guarantees only the authenticated person enters.
Yes, over 40% of our families reside in the UAE, US, UK, Singapore, and Canada. You can book seamlessly online or via WhatsApp, pay with international cards, and receive real-time notifications, photos, and post-visit reports formatted for your local timezone.
Hospitals and banks often experience unexpected delays. Your companion remains with your parent. Additional time beyond the booked slot is billed transparently in 30-minute intervals at our standard rate (₹100 per 30 mins) with zero penalty.
